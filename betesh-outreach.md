## Call script

**Opening**

"Hi, my name is Yehuda Wachmann. I'm calling about a patented baby product I'd like to submit to the Betesh Group. Who handles outside inventions or new product development?"

If the receptionist asks what it is: "It's a patented crawling bib for babies. I have a granted US utility patent and I'd like to send it to the right person."

**When you reach the right person (30 seconds)**

"Thank you for taking my call. My wife invented a full-length bib for crawling babies. It covers the chest, belly and legs, stays on with elastic straps that cross at the back, and has a waterproof inner layer. It has a granted US utility patent, number 11,986,026, issued in May 2024. We've sold over 500 units in Israel at about $33 retail, and it costs about $8 to make. We're looking to license it, or to sell the full package, to a company with US distribution. Do you take outside submissions, and what is the process?"

**Then ask for one next step**

- "Is there a submission form or an email address I should send it to?"
- "Do you need us to sign a submission agreement or NDA first? I'd like to read it before signing."
- "Can I send a sample and a short video?"

**Likely questions**

| They ask | You say |
| --- | --- |
| How much have you sold? | "Over 500 units in Israel, almost entirely through local print ads. The US and online markets are untested, which is why we want a partner with the channels." |
| Do you have a prototype? | "Yes, finished product. We also have about 200 units in stock and 19 rolls of production fabric." |
| Is it patented? | "Yes. Granted US utility patent 11,986,026 B2, with 14 claims, in force to about 2042. In Israel we have a registered design, and the patent application is pending." |
| Do you work through agents? | "We've also submitted through licensing agents. We're happy to work directly with you or through whoever you prefer." |
| What do you want for it? | "We're open on structure. A license with an advance and a royalty is our preference, or a sale of the full package. We'd like to hear how you usually structure these." |
| Who owns it? | "My wife is the inventor and owns the patent. I handle the business side for her." |

Do not say: "no competitor can copy it", or that the Israeli patent is granted.
Do not give a price on the first call. Let them name terms first.

**Closing**

"Great. I'll send the details to [name and email] today. Could I follow up in a week?"
